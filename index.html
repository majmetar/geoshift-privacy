Privacy Policy for GeoShift

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

GeoShift (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a free GPS location app published by AeroQuantix. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, what it sends to other services so its features work, and what it never does. GeoShift has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no user accounts.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, receive or store any of your information on our own servers. We do not operate any servers for this app.

2. Your Real Location

If you grant location permission, GeoShift reads your device’s real location on your device only, to show where you are on the map and to run the location service. Your real location is not transmitted by the app.

3. Information Sent to Third-Party Services

To provide maps, search and routes, the app sends the minimum information needed directly from your device to the services below. As with any internet request, these services also receive your device’s IP address. Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

FeatureWhat is sentService
Map displayRequests for the map tiles of the area you are viewingOpenFreeMap
SearchThe text you type and the approximate area of the map you are viewing (to rank nearby results first)Photon by komoot (OpenStreetMap data); your device’s built-in geocoder (on most phones, provided by Google)
Place namesThe coordinates of a point you select on the map, to look up its addressYour device’s built-in geocoder; Photon by komoot
Road routesThe coordinates of the stops in a route you createOSRM routing servers operated by FOSSGIS (routing.openstreetmap.de) and, as a backup for driving routes, the OSRM project (router.project-osrm.org)
Shared linksA short map link you share into the app, to expand it to its full addressThe link’s own website (for example, Google Maps)

None of this information is sold, used for advertising, or used to identify or track you.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Your saved places, recent places, settings and the last location you used are stored only on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account; that backup is controlled by you and Google, not by us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from the device.

5. Simulated Locations

When you turn on location spoofing, GeoShift provides the location you choose to your device so that other apps receive it. The simulated location is not sent to us. Other apps that read your location will receive the simulated location under their own privacy policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

GeoShift is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Security

All connections to the services listed above use encrypted HTTPS. Because we do not collect or store your data, there is no account or server-side data that could be exposed.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue at github.com/majmetar/geoshift-privacy/issues.
